TechMart Electronics - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are your store hours?
A: TechMart Electronics is open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM, and Sunday 10 AM to 6 PM.

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click Register on the top right of our website, enter your email and password, and verify your email address to activate your account.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Log in to your account and go to My Orders. Each order shows a tracking number and current status.

Q: Do you offer international shipping?
A: Yes, we ship to over 30 countries. International orders take 7-14 business days.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, PayPal, and net banking.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: Email support@techmart.com or call +1-800-555-0199, available 24/7.
