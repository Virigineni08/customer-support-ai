TechMart Electronics - Pricing and Plans

1. Product Pricing
All product prices are listed in USD and include applicable taxes for domestic orders.

2. TechMart Premium Subscription
Monthly Plan costs 9.99 dollars per month with free express shipping and early access to sales.
Annual Plan costs 89.99 dollars per year with the same benefits plus a free accessory bundle.

3. Price Match Guarantee
If you find a lower price for an identical product from an authorized retailer within 7 days of purchase, TechMart will refund the difference.

4. Bulk and Business Pricing
Businesses ordering 10 or more units of the same product qualify for a 10 percent bulk discount.
