TechMart Electronics - Shipping Policy

1. Domestic Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders above 50 dollars qualify for free standard shipping.

2. International Shipping
International orders take 7-14 business days depending on destination and customs processing.

3. Order Processing Time
Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days.

4. Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once the order ships.

5. Delayed or Lost Shipments
If a shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, contact support with your order number.
