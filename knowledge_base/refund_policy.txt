TechMart Electronics - Refund Policy

1. Eligibility
Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.

2. Refund Process
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

3. Damaged or Defective Items
If a product arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 48 hours of delivery for a free replacement or full refund.

4. Subscription Refunds
Premium subscription plans can be refunded within 7 days of purchase if the service was not used. After 7 days, subscriptions are non-refundable but can be cancelled.

5. How to Request a Refund
Go to My Orders, select the item, and click Request Refund. Alternatively, email refunds@techmart.com with your order number.
