TechMart Electronics - Warranty Policy

1. Standard Warranty
All electronics purchased from TechMart come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects and malfunctions under normal use.

2. What is Covered
Manufacturing defects, battery failure within the first year, screen malfunctions not caused by physical damage, and motherboard failure.

3. What is Not Covered
Physical damage caused by misuse, water damage, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear and tear.

4. How to Claim Warranty
Log in to your account, go to My Orders, select the product, and click Claim Warranty. Our technical team responds within 24-48 hours.
